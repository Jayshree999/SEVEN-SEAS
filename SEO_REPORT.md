## 2. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) & JSON-LD Schemas

To ensure AI chatbots and Google Rich Results understand the specific amenities and services you offer, we injected **Schema.org Structured Data** directly into the code of the site.

*   **Hotel & WebSite Schema**: Fully maps the hotel's 5-star rating, pricing `$$`, check-in/out times, and global search functionality.
*   **LodgingBusiness Schema**: Maps out individual premium rooms like the *Executive Suite* and *Royal Suite*.
*   **Restaurant Schema**: Explicitly lists *Salt All Day Dining* and *Thamburuu Kerala Restaurant*.
*   **HealthAndBeautyBusiness Schema**: Catalogs *Behnah Spa* and the *Gym*.
*   **NightClub Schema**: Properly categorizes *Baazigar* and *Vibe Nation* for nightlife searches.
*   **EventVenue Schema**: Highlights the *Mehfil Ballroom* for corporate events and weddings.

---

## 3. Sitemap & Technical Crawling

*   **Dynamic Sitemap (`sitemap.xml`)**: We deployed a highly structured sitemap that automatically feeds search engines all 20+ optimized routes with exact priorities (Homepage at 1.0, Rooms/Offers at 0.9, Dining/Entertainment at 0.8).
*   **Hardened `robots.txt`**: We secured your website by telling crawlers exactly which pages to index and strictly disallowing private directories (such as `/api/`, `/profile/`, and `/booking/`).
